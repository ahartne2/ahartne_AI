---
name: Outfit suggestion lifetime
description: Product constraints for rejection history and recovery from exhausted outfit options.
---

Treat rejected combinations as temporary browsing-session feedback, separate from accepted outfits and saved Dressed Wardrobe looks.

**Why:** The user requested no repeated rejected combinations during the same session, and later reported that outfits stopped loading. Persisting exhausted rejection history across reloads leaves otherwise valid outfit options hidden indefinitely.

**How to apply:** Allow a new browsing session or an explicit restart to show previously rejected options again. Never clear uploaded garments, accepted outfits, or saved Dressed Wardrobe looks to recover suggestions.
